Frequently asked questions
How long does it take to get my photos back?

Editing is one of my favorite parts! So you should have your photos back in 1 week, if your session is during a busier season then it might take 2 weeks, but no later!

What should I wear to the photo shoot?

Don’t worry, that is what I am here for! If you aren’t sure of what colors will look good or just need a friend to give you an honest opinion about what to wear, message me on Instagram or send me an email!

How will I receive my photos?

I deliver your photos through a high resolution online gallery through pixieset. From there, you have all the rights to print, download and share!

How long does it take to receive the final photos?

My goal is to enhance your photos while keeping them authentic and true to who you are.
